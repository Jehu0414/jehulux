BUSINESS PORTFOLIO - QUICK START

1. Open js/data.js.
2. Change businessName, profilePhoto, text, portfolio images, gallery images, and product information.
3. Replace each product "link" with your real affiliate URL.
4. Replace sample image URLs with your own image URLs or local paths such as:
   assets/images/profile.jpg
5. Open index.html in a browser.

There is NO admin panel and NO login.

Affiliate links open in a new tab. The site does not process payments; purchases happen on the external merchant website.

For publishing, upload the entire Business-Portfolio folder to your web host.
